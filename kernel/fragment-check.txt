Fragment check: every symbol in container.config against the final .config
=========================================================================

Produced by kernel/check-fragment.py, which parses the fragment and the final
config and compares them symbol by symbol, and additionally looks each symbol up
in the kernel tree's Kconfig files so a symbol that does not exist is reported as
"NO SUCH SYMBOL" rather than as a silent drop.

  python3 check-fragment.py container.config linux-7.2.6/.config linux-7.2.6

Config: Linux 7.2.6, ARCH=riscv, arch/riscv/configs/defconfig + container.config,
then `make olddefconfig`.

Result: 141 of 141 symbols got the value the fragment asked for. Nothing dropped.

Two symbols were removed from the fragment after the first run reported them as
NO SUCH SYMBOL in 7.2.6, and the reasons are written into container.config:

  * CONFIG_CGROUP_NS: no such Kconfig symbol. Cgroup namespaces are built
    unconditionally, kernel/cgroup/Makefile has namespace.o in obj-y.
  * CONFIG_CRYPTO_GHASH: folded into CONFIG_CRYPTO_LIB_GF128HASH
    (lib/crypto/Kconfig), which CONFIG_CRYPTO_GCM selects. It is =y in the final
    config under the new name.
